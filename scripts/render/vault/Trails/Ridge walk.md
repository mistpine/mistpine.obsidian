The ridge trail leaves the valley at the old sawmill and climbs through
[[Scots pine]] and [[Norway spruce]] until the trees thin out above the fog
line. On still mornings the valley fills with cloud and only the tops of the
pines show through, which is the whole reason to start before sunrise.

## Route

1. Sawmill car park to the first bench, **2.1 km**, steady climb.
2. Bench to the saddle, *mostly level*, boggy after rain.
3. Saddle to the summit cairn, steep and rocky for the last 300 m.

- [x] Pack the thermos
- [x] Check the forecast in [[Fog forecast]]
- [ ] Photograph the cairn at first light

> [!tip] Best time
> Leave by 05:30 in October: the fog usually sits below 700 m until about
> nine, and the summit is above it.

> [!warning] Footing
> The granite slabs past the saddle are ==slick when wet==. Poles help.
