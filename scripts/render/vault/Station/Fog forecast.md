Valley fog forms on clear, still nights when the air cools to its dew point.
The [[Weather station]] flags it when the gap is under 2.5 °C.

> [!note]
> Readings from October are in the station log. The [[Ridge walk]] is best
> on mornings after a flagged night.

> [!danger] Not a safety forecast
> Use the national forecast for anything that matters.
